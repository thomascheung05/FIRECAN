A Record of Canada's Historic Forest Fires

FIRECAN explores historical forest fires across Canada.
Filter by Province, Year, Size, Distance From A Location, Watershed, and Custom Polygons.
Increase Polygon Resolution in Display Details.
Export your data for future use.

Use the Watershed Explorer to discover watersheds across Canada and use them to filter.
Upload your own GeoJSON files to display fires within an area.
To decrease wait times, default polygon resolution is low. Decrease the Polygon Tolerance to get more accurate fire perimeters.
Exporting data uses current filter settings and has maximum resolution.

Data for FIRECAN comes from:

  • Données Québec · Quebec fires
  • Canadian Wildland Fire Information System · Canadian fire records
  • Esri Canada · Canadian watersheds

Historical records vary in coverage and completeness. Consult the source datasets for their details.
